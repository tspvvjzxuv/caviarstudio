**Document version:** v1

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Weby, videá a fotky pre podniky: aby vás ľudia našli, uvideli a zavolali.
**What it does:** Caviar is a small studio in Trnava that makes a one-page website, short vertical reels, and edited photos so a local business can be found, seen, and called. Work is delivered in days, not months. Files belong to the client after payment.
**Product category:** Local creative studio / web + photo + video for small businesses
**Product type:** Service (project packages)
**Business model:** One-off packages, launch prices including VAT, plus monthly marketing retainers. Foto Mini 79 EUR, Video Reel 59 EUR (three reels 129 EUR), Web Jednostránka 219 EUR, Caviar Komplet 379 EUR, E-shop 590 EUR one-time plus 19 EUR/month (server, backups, maintenance). Monthly: Social Štart 249 EUR, Social Plus 449 EUR, Marketing Komplet 690 EUR (ad budget paid to Meta and Google; minimum 3 months).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Restaurants, lodging, services, online shops, craftspeople, and small firms in Trnava and nearby.
**Decision-makers:** Owner or manager who can approve a small marketing spend without a committee.
**Primary use case:** Need a site people can find and call from, plus photos and a short video of the place.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get a page live with a contact button, map, and basic SEO
- Show what the place looks like in a 15–30 s reel
- Have usable photos for web, menu, shop, and socials
**Use cases:**
- Launch or replace a one-page site
- Shoot on location, then hand over edited files

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The business is hard to find or looks unfinished online, so people do not call.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- DIY builders leave the owner stuck with setup, SEO, and visuals
- Large agencies are slow and priced for bigger brands
**What it costs them:** Missed calls and bookings. Exact numbers are not claimed.
**Emotional tension:** Looking amateur next to newer places nearby.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other local web / photo / video studios — no named competitor list is on file.
**Secondary:** Website builders and freelance marketplaces — the owner still has to brief, shoot, and stitch the look.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing / posting phone photos — cheap, inconsistent, easy to ignore.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One visual language across web, video, and photo
- Launch prices (úvodná cena), all including VAT
- Reply right away; web in 5–7 days; photo/video 3–5 days after the shoot
- Two revision rounds in every package, plus small fixes for 2 weeks after handover; files belong to the client after payment
**How we do it differently:** One studio, one look, Trnava and nearby, no invented case studies.
**Why that's better:** The owner does not have to coordinate three vendors.
**Why customers choose us:** Speed, a clear package, and a single visual.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Hosting is extra | Domain is connected. For the one-pager, hosting can stay with the client, or a cheap option is named — it is not in the web price unless agreed. The e-shop includes its own server for 19 EUR/month. |
| How many edits? | Two rounds are included, plus small fixes for 2 weeks after handover. Further rounds are agreed up front. |
| Who owns the files? | After payment, the client owns the site, photos, and video. |

**Anti-persona:** Enterprises that need a CMS or a multi-page product catalog.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Current site or photos do not get calls.
**Pull:** One package, one look, a date they can calendar.
**Habit:** Posting whatever is on the phone.
**Anxiety:** Hidden fees, slow delivery, losing the files. Answered in the FAQ.
